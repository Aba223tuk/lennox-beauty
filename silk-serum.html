What it is

A mist, not an oil.

Questions

Everything else

Will this make my hair greasy?

No — it's a weightless leave-in mist, not an oil you pour on. The fine spray lays down a very thin, even layer that dries silky instead of sitting on top of your hair. If coconut or castor oil burned you before, this feels completely different.

Does it work on curly, textured, or color-treated hair?

Yes. It smooths the cuticle without relaxing your pattern — curls stay curls, minus the halo of frizz. Safe for color-treated hair.

How long until I see results?

Flyaways smooth immediately. The full effect — hair that stays smooth through humid days — builds over 2–4 weeks of regular use. That's why most people take the 3-bottle set.

Does it have a scent?

Yes, but lightly — rose. You catch it when you first mist, and it fades to something faint rather than following you round the day. Two of the twenty-two people who rated our manufacturer's listing tagged it “no fragrance”, so treat it as barely-there. It won't fight anything you wear over it.
