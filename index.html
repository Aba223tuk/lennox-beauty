Our story

Questions People Ask Before They Buy.

What does Lennox make?

Four things today: the Smoothing Roll-On, for the look of bumps after shaving or waxing; the Lash Kit, DIY lash clusters; the Nail Drill, a cordless drill for at-home manicures; and the Silk Serum, a daily anti-frizz mist. All four are on the catalog.

When will my order arrive?

Dispatched within 1–2 business days and typically arriving 7–13 days later, tracked the whole way. You get the tracking number by email the moment it ships.

What's the guarantee?

30 days on everything (60 on the Roll-On). If it isn't for you, email Lennoxsupport@gmail.com and we refund every cent — you don't ship anything back.

Is there a subscription?

No. Nothing here renews. Multi-packs cost less per unit because you bought more, not because you joined anything.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. US only for now — we'd rather do one country properly than take money for a parcel we can't track to your door.

Why only four products?

A product goes up when there's a reason for it to exist, not to fill a shelf.
