Questions

FAQs

What does Lennox actually make?

Three things today: the Lash Kit, DIY lash clusters you apply at home; the Nail Drill, a cordless drill for at-home manicures; and the Silk Serum, a daily leave-in mist for frizz. More are in development and we'd rather show them late than early. All three are on the catalog.

When will my order arrive?

Dispatched within 1–2 business days and typically arriving 7–13 days later, tracked the whole way. You get the tracking number by email the moment it ships.

What's the guarantee?

30 days on everything. If it isn't for you, email Lennoxsupport@gmail.com and we refund every cent — you don't ship anything back, and there's no form to fill in.

Is there a subscription?

No. Nothing here renews, and nothing is cheaper only if you sign up for something. Multi-packs cost less per unit because you bought more, not because you joined anything.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. US only for now — we'd rather do one country properly than take money for a parcel we can't track to your door.

Why are there only three products?

Because a product goes up when there's a reason for it to exist, not to fill a shelf. When we don't have a good version of something yet, the card says "in development" and stays that way until it's real.
