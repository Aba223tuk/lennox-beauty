Questions.

Will it sting?

It is an acid formula, so it can tingle on thin skin for a minute. Try it on a small patch inside your arm first, keep it off fresh nicks and broken skin, and away from your eyes. If it stings properly, rinse it off — and email us, because that is a refund.

Where can I use it?

Bikini line, underarms, legs — anywhere you shave or wax, and anywhere the bumps keep coming back in the same place. Not your face, not broken skin.

When will it arrive, and where from?

It ships direct from the warehouse that packs it, free and tracked. Dispatched in 1 to 2 business days, delivered 7 to 13 days after that. The real dates are above the buttons, counted from today.

What if it does nothing for me?

Sixty days from the day it arrives. Every cent back and you keep it — no returns to mail, no forms. Email Lennoxsupport@gmail.com.

What will the box look like?

Plain. It comes in the maker's own box and the name Lennox is not on it, one parcel, tracked. We would rather tell you now than have you open it and wonder.

Why two bottles?

Pairs are the only way we sell it. A 50ml roller in this aisle runs $18 to $52 on its own; two of ours is $17.50 each and three is $14.99. One tends to live in the shower and the other in a bag.

Pregnant, breastfeeding, or already using acids?

Ask your doctor before putting salicylic acid on skin, and do not layer it over another acid on the same patch. We cannot answer that one for you.
