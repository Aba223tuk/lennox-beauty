About

We only wanted to make one thing.

Lennox Beauty isn't a range and it isn't a shelf. It's one product we cared enough about to get right — sold by people who still pick up the phone.

Why we bothered

Walk down any hair aisle and most of what's sold against frizz is a conditioner with a new label and a bigger promise. Shelves of it. We didn't want to add to that pile, so we didn't launch a range. We put everything into one bottle and left it there.

That choice costs us. A single product is harder to sell, harder to market, and there's nowhere to hide if it isn't good. We think that's the point — it keeps us honest in a way a catalogue never would.

What we won't do

We won't buy reviews, and we won't put a follower count on a page as though it were proof. We won't invent a heritage — we're new, and saying so plainly is cheaper than pretending otherwise. And we won't show you a before-and-after of two different people's hair, which is most of what you'll find in this category once you start looking.

Everything we claim here is either something you can check yourself or something we'll refund you for. That's the entire standard, and we'd rather be held to it than admired for a story.

The promise

Use it for 30 days. If it doesn't do what we said it would, we refund every cent and you keep the bottles — nothing to mail back, no forms, no restocking fee. We'd rather lose the money than have you feel had.

And if you'd rather speak to someone, (929) 670-9555 is a direct line. Text it or call it. A person answers — it isn't a ticket queue.

Where this goes

Slowly, and only if the first thing earns it. We'd rather have one product people genuinely run out of and reorder than five they tried once and forgot. If we ever make something else, it'll be because someone asked us for it — not because a supplier had stock to move.
