About

Beauty shouldn't have to cost a fortune.

A beauty brand for people who got tired of paying a lot for very little — and I'd rather earn that slowly than sell you a shelf of things that don't work.

Why I started it

I started Lennox because I genuinely love beauty. Not the packaging — the effect. The way the right product changes how someone carries themselves for the rest of the day. That's a small thing, and it also isn't a small thing at all.

What I couldn't get past was the cost. I watched how quickly a simple routine turns into hundreds of dollars of products you barely use — a single bottle running you the price of a night out and still not doing what the label promised. Women absorb that, over and over, on products that were never going to work in the first place.

So I wanted a brand built around products that actually earn a place in your routine, without making you buy ten things to solve one problem. Not cheap — I'm not interested in cheap. Honest about what something costs to make, and about what it's genuinely worth to you.

How we decide what to make

Walk down any beauty aisle and a lot of what's on it is the same formula behind a new label and a bigger promise. Shelves of it. We're not interested in adding to that pile just to have more things to sell.

So everything we put our name on has to clear the same bar before it goes up: does it genuinely do what we say it does, and is it priced so that trying it doesn't feel like a gamble? If something can't clear both, it doesn't ship — however much easier it would be to launch it anyway.

What we won't do

We won't buy reviews, and we won't put a follower count on a page as though it were proof. We won't invent a heritage — we're new, and saying so plainly is cheaper than pretending otherwise. And we won't show you a before-and-after of two different people's hair, which is most of what you'll find in beauty once you start looking.

Everything we claim here is either something you can check yourself or something we'll refund you for. That's the entire standard, and we'd rather be held to it than admired for a story.

The promise

Try anything we make for 30 days. If it doesn't do what we said it would, we refund every cent and you keep what you were sent — nothing to mail back, no forms, no restocking fee. We'd rather lose the money than have you feel had.

And if you'd rather speak to someone, (929) 670-9555 is a direct line. Text it or call it, or email Lennoxsupport@gmail.com. A person answers — it isn't a ticket queue.

What comes next

More. We're building this range out properly rather than all at once, and every addition has to clear the same bar as everything already here. We'd rather sell you three things you genuinely run out of and reorder than thirty you tried once and forgot.

What we add gets decided by what people actually ask us for — not by whatever a supplier has sitting in a warehouse. If there's something you wish existed at a price that isn't insulting, tell us. That's a real invitation, and the number below reaches a person.
