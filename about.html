What the Silk Serum actually does

It's a featherweight leave-in mist that lays the hair cuticle flat. A flat cuticle is what cuts frizz, kills static and puts the shine back — it's the whole mechanism, and it's why the finish is smooth rather than coated.

Two or three sprays over mid-lengths and ends, comb it through, then air dry or blow-dry as usual. No rinsing. Under a minute. A 100ml bottle lasts most people two to three months of daily use.
