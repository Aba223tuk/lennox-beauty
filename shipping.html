Shipping

Last updated 19 September 2026.

Free US shipping on everything, tracked the whole way, dispatched within 1–2 business days.

What it costs

Nothing, anywhere in the United States, with no minimum order. The price you see is the price you pay — there is no shipping line added at checkout.

When it arrives

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days and typically reach you 7–13 days after that. You get a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves.

We do not offer a paid express tier. There is no genuinely faster service available to us at the moment, and selling one we could not deliver would be a promise we would break on the first order.

Parcels that arrive separately

Some orders travel in more than one parcel — a kit and its free Silk Serum come from different places, so the serum usually arrives first and the kit follows. Nothing is missing; the second parcel has its own tracking.

Where we ship

The United States, including Alaska, Hawaii and APO/FPO addresses. We do not ship internationally yet.

Your guarantee starts when it arrives

The 30-day money-back guarantee runs from the day the order reaches you, not from the day you place it. Time in transit never eats into it. How the guarantee works →

If tracking stalls

If a parcel stops moving, or is marked delivered and you do not have it, tell us. We chase the carrier, and if it cannot be found we replace or refund the order. You are not left holding a tracking number.

Wrong address

Message us before dispatch and we will fix it. After dispatch we cannot redirect a parcel in flight, but if it comes back to us we reship it free.
