How to use it
  1. Start clean and dry. No lotion, no oil, no powder. Adhesive needs bare skin to last an evening.
  2. Cut what you need. The paper backing is gridded, so you cut against a line instead of guessing.
  3. Anchor low, pull up and in. Start under the bust, pull toward the opposite shoulder, press for ten seconds. Repeat on the other side.
  4. Take it off in the shower. Warm water for two minutes, then peel slowly along the skin — never against it, and never dry.

What it is

For the outfit a bra cannot go under.

A roll of skin-safe adhesive tape, five metres long, that you cut to length and stick to yourself instead of wearing a bra. It carries the weight through the tape rather than through straps, which is why it works under a backless dress, a deep V, or anything cut so that a bra would show.

It is a single-wear thing. You cut what you need, wear it for an evening, soak it off and throw those pieces away — the rest of the roll keeps. Depending on cup size and how much lift you build, one roll is roughly five to eight wears.

What it is not: shapewear, a bra replacement for everyday use, or something to sleep in.

Shade guide

Picking your shade

Match your under-bust

Not your face. Your torso usually runs a shade lighter and cooler than the skin you see in the mirror, and the torso is where the tape actually sits.

Between two shades? Go darker

Tape slightly darker than skin reads as shadow. Tape slightly lighter reads as tape.

Black isn't only for deep skin

Under a black backless dress, black tape disappears on everybody — there, match the fabric rather than yourself.

Questions

Everything else

Will it hold me?

Up to a DD+ with the wide roll. Below a C cup you will use less than half a roll per wear, so the narrow one goes a long way. One roll is roughly five to eight wears depending on cup size and how much lift you build.

Does it hurt to take off?

Not if you soak it first. Two minutes under warm water, then peel slowly with the skin rather than against it. Dry-ripping is what people mean when they say body tape hurts.

I have sensitive skin.

Patch-test on your inner arm for twenty minutes before you commit to a whole evening. This is an adhesive, and a small number of people react to any adhesive whatever it is made of.

What it's made of

Polyethylene backing, medical-grade acrylic pressure-sensitive adhesive, silicone-coated release liner.

Latex-free. It is an adhesive, not a skincare product — nothing in it is meant to stay on your skin, and it all comes off with warm water. Read the California disclosure at the bottom of this page before you buy.
