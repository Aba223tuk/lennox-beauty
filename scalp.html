What it is

A nine-session scalp course in a box. Each 5 ml ampoule is sealed on its own. The micro-infusion head screws onto whichever ampoule you are using, so the little bottle becomes the handle — there is no separate device, nothing to charge, and nothing to decant into anything.

It is for people who have run out of things to put on their hair. The part line is the first thing you look at in the mirror and the last thing a conditioner can do anything about — it is scalp, and scalp is not what conditioner is for. This is a scalp product. Everything else you own is a hair product.

How it works

The head carries micro-needles 0.2 mm long — shorter than a credit card is thick. Under 0.3 mm is cosmetic depth: the shallow end of micro-needling, the part that belongs in a bathroom rather than a clinic. That is a deliberate choice, and it cuts both ways. It is why this needs no numbing, no appointment and no recovery day. It is also why it is not going to do what a clinical treatment does.

As you roll along a parting, serum feeds out of the ampoule through the head and goes in at the surface of the scalp rather than sitting on the hair above it.

What's in the box

  • 9 × 5 ml ampoules — 45 ml of copper peptide serum in total, each one sealed until you open it.
  • 1 micro-infusion head — 0.2 mm, screws onto any ampoule in the tray.

One ampoule per session, so a box is nine sessions.

The formula

Three actives, and a reason for each.

  1. Tripeptide-1 Copper. The INCI name for GHK-Cu, or blue copper peptide — it is what makes the serum blue. It conditions the scalp and helps keep it stable.
  2. Caffeine. Revitalises the scalp.
  3. Panax Ginseng Root Extract. Replenishes nutrients and supports scalp health.

Copper peptides degrade once they meet air, which is the real reason this is nine sealed ampoules and not one big bottle going flat on a shelf. Use within three months of opening.

How to use it

  1. Start clean and dry. Wash, dry properly, and do it on a scalp with nothing else on it.
  2. Screw the head onto one ampoule. The bottle is now the handle.
  3. Roll slowly along a parting. Section by section, two or three drops each. Let the weight of the head do it — pressing harder does not send it deeper, it just hurts.
  4. Massage it in and leave it. Fingertips, no rinsing. Go to bed.

One ampoule per session. Afterwards, rinse the head under hot water and let it dry completely before it goes back in the tray. It is yours and nobody else's.

Everything else

Will this regrow my hair?

We can't promise that. The clinical work on microneedling for hair used depths of 0.5 mm and above, and nearly all of it paired the needling with minoxidil — the approved drug doing the work. This kit is 0.2 mm and there is no drug in it. What it does is condition the scalp and put a copper peptide serum into its surface. If you are losing hair, see a doctor: there are treatments approved for exactly that, and this is not one of them.

Does it hurt?

At 0.2 mm most people describe it as prickly rather than painful — closer to a stiff brush than a needle. Roll slowly, never press, and stop if your scalp is sore. A little redness that settles within the hour is ordinary; anything more than that means stop.

Who should not use it

Patch test on a small area first. Not for broken skin, sunburn, active irritation, an inflamed or infected scalp, or over moles. One head, one person — never share it, and replace it if the surface is ever visibly damaged. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or already on a prescription scalp treatment, ask your doctor before you start.
