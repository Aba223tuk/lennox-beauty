Returns & Refunds

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Thirty days, every cent back, nothing to mail and no form to fill in. If it is not for you, one message ends it.

The 30-day guarantee

Use it for 30 days from the day it arrives. If it is not for you — for any reason, or no reason — tell us and we refund the full amount you paid, shipping included.

You do not have to send anything back. Posting a part-used cosmetic across the country costs more than the product and helps nobody, so we do not ask for it. Keep it, pass it on, or throw it away.

How to claim

  • Text or call (929) 670-9555, or email Lennoxsupport@gmail.com.
  • Tell us the name the order was placed in, or the order number if it is handy.
  • That is the whole process. There is no form, no returns portal, and nobody will ask you three times why you are leaving.

When you get the money

Refunds go back to the card or wallet that paid, usually the same day we hear from you. Your bank then takes its own time — normally 5–10 business days for the money to appear.

The free Silk Serum

Kits ship with a free Silk Serum. If you refund the kit, keep the serum. We do not deduct it and we do not ask for it back.

If something arrives damaged, wrong or missing

Email a photo to Lennoxsupport@gmail.com and we replace it or refund it, your choice. This is not limited to 30 days and it does not use up your guarantee.

If it never arrives

If tracking stalls, or a parcel is marked delivered and you do not have it, tell us. We chase the carrier, and if it cannot be found we replace or refund the order. You are not left holding a tracking number.

Cancelling before it ships

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days. Message us before then and we cancel and refund outright. After dispatch it becomes an ordinary guarantee claim, which reaches the same place.

Exchanges

We do not run an exchange process, because the refund is faster. Ask for the refund and place the order you actually wanted — or tell us what you were after and we will get it to you and sort the money out afterwards.

Your rights

This guarantee is on top of the rights you already have under your state's law, not instead of them. Nothing here takes any of them away.
