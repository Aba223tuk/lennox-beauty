How to use the Silk Serum

Start with dry or towel-dried hair. Mist 2–3 sprays evenly over mid-lengths and ends from about a hand's width away, comb through, then air dry or blow-dry as usual. No rinsing — it's a leave-in. Takes about a minute.

What it is, and how it works

A leave-in smoothing mist (100ml spray bottle) that lays the hair cuticle flat, cutting flyaways and static while adding shine. The micro-fine nozzle throws a roughly 54-micron mist — finer than a human hair — so it spreads evenly instead of landing in wet patches, which is what makes a spray feel weightless rather than greasy.

Most people see smoother hair from the first application, with the full stay-smooth-all-day effect building over 2–4 weeks. Non-greasy, dry-touch finish, and a soft fragrance that lingers in your hair through the day.
