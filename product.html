What it is

A featherweight leave-in mist that lays the hair cuticle flat — cutting flyaways and static, adding shine, and leaving a soft scent that lasts through the day. It is a spray, not an oil, so it finishes dry to the touch instead of greasy.

The nozzle throws a roughly 54-micron mist, finer than a human hair, so it spreads evenly rather than landing in wet patches. Most people notice smoother hair from the first application, with the full stay-smooth effect building over two to four weeks. Safe for curly, textured and colour-treated hair.

How to use it

  1. Mist. Two or three sprays over mid-lengths and ends, from about a hand's width away. Dry or towel-dried hair, not soaking.
  2. Comb through. So it spreads evenly instead of sitting in one place.
  3. Dry as usual. Air dry or blow-dry. No rinsing — it's a leave-in. The whole thing takes about a minute.

Everything else

Rose scent. No added colour. If you are sensitive to fragrance, patch test on a small section first.
